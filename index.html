THE ART OF HIDDEN
A secret in three steps.
  1. Choose an image. A larger image has more room for your payload. Transparent covers are flattened onto white to keep exported pixels reliable.
  2. Add your secret. Type a message or choose a file or audio clip, then enter a passphrase of at least 8 characters.
  3. Download and share the PNG. Share the passphrase separately. Reveal data recovers the secret from the unedited PNG.
This web edition uses the STGWEB01 format with AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2-SHA256. Desktop and upstream image formats are not compatible. There is no passphrase recovery. Encryption protects the content; steganography does not guarantee the presence of hidden data is undetectable.
